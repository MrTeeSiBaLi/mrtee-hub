@cards Glossary · Vocabulary from Paper 001
@card accommodate | **chứa đủ chỗ cho** · v. /əˈkɒmədeɪt/ — Have enough space for someone or something. | The hall can accommodate sixty guests. (accommodate people; no preposition)
@card acoustic | **thuộc âm thanh** · adj. /əˈkuːstɪk/ — Connected with sound or its transmission. | The designer tested the acoustic properties of the hall. (acoustic + noun)
@card acute | **rất nhạy bén** · adj. /əˈkjuːt/ — Highly developed or strongly felt. | Her acute sense of smell helped her identify the ingredient. (acute sense of; different from cute)
@card apprenticeship | **thời gian học nghề** · n. /əˈprentɪsʃɪp/ — A period of learning skilled work through practice. | He completed an apprenticeship with a furniture maker. (an apprenticeship in/with)
@card array | **dãy hoặc tập hợp** · n. /əˈreɪ/ — An organised arrangement or a wide selection. | The exhibition offered an array of different materials. (an array of; plural arrays)
@card autobiography | **tự truyện** · n. /ˌɔːtəbaɪˈɒɡrəfi/ — A person’s written account of their own life. | Her autobiography begins with her school years. (an autobiography; autobiographical adj.)
@card cacophony | **âm thanh hỗn tạp chói tai** · n. /kəˈkɒfəni/ — A mixture of loud, harsh sounds. | A cacophony of bells woke the village. (a cacophony of; formal/descriptive)
@card collective | **chung, mang tính tập thể** · adj. /kəˈlektɪv/ — Shared or produced by people acting together. | The garden reflects a collective effort. (collective responsibility/effort)
@card commend | **khen ngợi** · v. /kəˈmend/ — Express approval of an action or achievement. | The coach commended their careful preparation. (commend somebody for something)
@card conscientiously | **một cách tận tâm** · adv. /ˌkɒnʃiˈenʃəsli/ — With care and a strong sense of duty. | She conscientiously checked each reference. (from conscientious adj.)
@card convey | **truyền đạt** · v. /kənˈveɪ/ — Communicate an idea or impression. | The photograph conveys a sense of isolation. (convey something to somebody)
@card corrugated | **có gân sóng** · adj. /ˈkɒrəɡeɪtɪd/ — Shaped into repeated parallel ridges. | The shed had a corrugated roof. (corrugated iron/cardboard)
@card dedication | **sự tận tâm** · n. /ˌdedɪˈkeɪʃən/ — Sustained commitment to an activity. | Her dedication to practice impressed the team. (dedication to + noun/gerund)
@card defensive | **có thái độ tự vệ** · adj. /dɪˈfensɪv/ — Quick to protect oneself from criticism. | He became defensive when asked about the delay. (feel/become defensive)
@card discrepancy | **sự không khớp** · n. /dɪˈskrepənsi/ — A difference between records expected to agree. | We found a discrepancy between the two invoices. (a discrepancy between/in)
@card elegy | **khúc tưởng niệm, lời thương tiếc** · n. /ˈelədʒi/ — A work expressing sorrow or a sense of loss. | The poem is an elegy for a lost landscape. (an elegy for/to; literary)
@card emit | **phát ra** · v. /ɪˈmɪt/ — Send out sound, light or another substance. | The device emits a soft green light. (emit–emitted–emitting)
@card enforce | **thực thi, buộc tuân thủ** · v. /ɪnˈfɔːs/ — Make people follow a rule. | The school will enforce the new rule fairly. (enforce a rule/law)
@card illuminate | **làm sáng tỏ** · v. /ɪˈluːmɪneɪt/ — Make a subject clearer or easier to understand. | The discussion illuminated a difficult issue. (illuminate something; formal)
@card incidental | **phụ, có vẻ không quan trọng** · adj. /ˌɪnsɪˈdentəl/ — Secondary to what initially seems important. | The incidental details made the account vivid. (incidental to; incidental detail)
@card indicator | **chỉ báo** · n. /ˈɪndɪkeɪtə/ — A measure used to suggest a wider condition. | The sign is an indicator of recent activity. (an indicator of)
@card intervention | **biện pháp can thiệp** · n. /ˌɪntəˈvenʃən/ — An action intended to change a situation. | The team evaluated the intervention after six weeks. (intervention in; an intervention)
@card intuition | **trực giác** · n. /ˌɪntʃuːˈɪʃən/ — Understanding reached without explicit analysis. | Her intuition suggested a different approach. (intuition about; intuitive adj.)
@card lame | **bị khập khiễng** · adj. /leɪm/ — Unable to walk normally because of an injured leg or foot. | The animal was lame after the fall. (physically descriptive in this context)
@card picturesque | **đẹp như tranh** · adj. /ˌpɪktʃəˈresk/ — Visually attractive in a distinctive way. | They stayed in a picturesque coastal village. (picturesque scene/village)
@card qualified | **có đủ chuyên môn** · adj. /ˈkwɒlɪfaɪd/ — Having the training needed for a role. | A qualified technician inspected the equipment. (qualified to do; qualified for)
@card resilience | **khả năng vượt khó, phục hồi** · n. /rɪˈzɪljəns/ — The capacity to recover or continue after difficulty. | The team showed resilience after the setback. (show/build resilience; resilient adj.)
@card rickety | **xiêu vẹo, ọp ẹp** · adj. /ˈrɪkəti/ — Weak and likely to shake or break. | A rickety wooden ladder stood against the shed. (rickety chair/stairs/structure)
@card self-serving | **vụ lợi, thiên về lợi ích bản thân** · adj. /ˌselfˈsɜːvɪŋ/ — Advancing one’s own interests rather than a stated purpose. | His self-serving explanation persuaded nobody. (usually disapproving)
@card traceable | **có thể truy nguyên** · adj. /ˈtreɪsəbəl/ — Able to be followed back to a source or record. | Every component is traceable to a supplier. (traceable to; untraceable opposite)
@card unexamined | **chưa được xem xét kỹ** · adj. /ˌʌnɪɡˈzæmɪnd/ — Accepted without careful consideration. | An unexamined assumption can mislead a team. (unexamined assumption/belief)
