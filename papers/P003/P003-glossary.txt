@cards Glossary · Vocabulary from Paper 003
@card accountability | **trách nhiệm giải trình** · n. /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti/ — The obligation to explain and take responsibility for decisions. | Clear records help maintain accountability in the organisation. (accountability for; formal)
@card administrative | **thuộc công việc hành chính** · adj. /ədˈmɪnɪstrətɪv/ — Relating to the organisation of routine work. | The new assistant handles administrative tasks. (administrative work/costs)
@card attractive | **hấp dẫn** · adj. /əˈtræktɪv/ — Appealing or likely to interest someone. | The salary made the position attractive to graduates. (attractive to; abstract as well as visual uses)
@card audit | **kiểm tra có hệ thống** · v. /ˈɔːdɪt/ — Examine records or procedures systematically. | An outside team will audit the safety procedures. (audit a process; noun: an audit)
@card census | **cuộc điều tra, đếm toàn bộ** · n. /ˈsensəs/ — An official count of every member of a population. | The village census recorded every household. (carry out / take a census; countable)
@card chronology | **trình tự niên đại** · n. /krəˈnɒlədʒi/ — An ordered account or sequence of events in time. | The notes provide a chronology of the negotiations. (a chronology of; do not confuse with duration)
@card conclusive | **có tính kết luận; xác quyết** · adj. /kənˈkluːsɪv/ — Strong enough to settle a question. | The available results are interesting but not conclusive. (conclusive evidence; not merely persuasive)
@card conservator | **chuyên gia bảo tồn hiện vật** · n. /kənˈsɜːvətə(r)/ — A specialist who cares for historic objects or artworks. | A conservator examined the fragile manuscript. (Different from a political conservative.)
@card contest | **phản bác; khiếu nại** · v. /kənˈtest/ — Formally question whether a claim or decision is right. | The residents can contest the decision in writing. (Verb stress on second syllable.)
@card coordinate | **điều phối, phối hợp** · v. /kəʊˈɔːdɪneɪt/ — Organise people or groups so that they work together effectively. | Two teachers coordinated the school trip. (coordinate a project; coordinate with someone)
@card curator | **người phụ trách bộ sưu tập; giám tuyển** · n. /kjʊəˈreɪtə(r)/ — A person who organises or manages a collection or exhibition. | The curator selected six photographs for the display. (curator of a collection)
@card defective | **có lỗi; khiếm khuyết** · adj. /dɪˈfektɪv/ — Not working properly or containing a fault. | A defective cable interrupted the signal. (defective equipment/reasoning)
@card endorse | **tán thành; phê chuẩn** · v. /ɪnˈdɔːs/ — Express approval of a proposal or decision. | The committee declined to endorse the proposal. (endorse a recommendation; formal)
@card exposed | **trống trải; không được che chắn** · adj. /ɪkˈspəʊzd/ — Unprotected from weather or other outside conditions. | The shelter stood on an exposed hillside. (exposed to wind; context-dependent)
@card extrapolate | **ngoại suy** · v. /ɪkˈstræpəleɪt/ — Use limited observations to infer a wider result. | We cannot extrapolate from a single busy weekend. (extrapolate from; technical/formal)
@card factual | **thuộc sự kiện; có tính thực tế** · adj. /ˈfæktʃuəl/ — Concerned with facts rather than opinions. | Please correct factual errors before submitting the report. (factual error/information)
@card gloss | **độ bóng bề mặt** · n. /ɡlɒs/ — A smooth shine on a surface. | The varnish gave the wooden box a soft gloss. (This sense differs from a brief explanation.)
@card hazard | **mối nguy** · n. /ˈhæzəd/ — Something that may cause harm. | Loose cables are a hazard in a crowded room. (a hazard to; safety hazard)
@card headland | **mũi đất nhô ra biển** · n. /ˈhedlənd/ — An area of land projecting into the sea. | A lighthouse stood on the headland. (Countable geographical noun.)
@card hypothetical | **giả định** · adj. /ˌhaɪpəˈθetɪkl/ — Imagined in order to examine a possibility. | The trainer presented a hypothetical disagreement. (hypothetical case/situation)
@card index | **chỉ số** · n. /ˈɪndeks/ — A figure that shows how something changes over time, rather than its exact size or amount. | The price index rose slightly last month. (an index of something; plural indexes or indices)
@card institutional | **thuộc tổ chức và cơ chế của tổ chức** · adj. /ˌɪnstɪˈtjuːʃənl/ — Relating to an organisation and its established practices. | The project needs institutional support to continue. (institutional change/practice)
@card intervene | **can thiệp** · v. /ˌɪntəˈviːn/ — Become involved to change a situation. | The teacher intervened when the discussion became personal. (intervene in; intervention (n.))
@card intuition | **trực giác** · n. /ˌɪntjuˈɪʃn/ — An immediate judgement made without explicit reasoning. | Her intuition suggested a simpler explanation. (intuition about; intuitive (adj.))
@card itinerary | **lịch trình** · n. /aɪˈtɪnərəri/ — A plan showing the places and stages of a journey. | We changed our itinerary after the ferry was cancelled. (plan/change an itinerary)
@card marsh | **đầm lầy** · n. /mɑːʃ/ — An area of soft, wet, low land. | Herons were feeding in the marsh. (countable; also marshland (uncountable))
@card radiosonde | **thiết bị thám không vô tuyến** · n. /ˈreɪdiəʊsɒnd/ — A small instrument carried by a weather balloon that sends back measurements. | The radiosonde sent data back throughout its flight. (technical term; countable)
@card rugged | **gồ ghề, hiểm trở** · adj. /ˈrʌɡɪd/ — (of land) Rough, steep and difficult to cross. | The rugged coast was hard to reach by car. (rugged terrain/coastline/mountains)
@card shortlist | **đưa vào danh sách tuyển chọn** · v. /ˈʃɔːtlɪst/ — Choose a smaller set of candidates for further consideration. | Five applicants were shortlisted for interview. (shortlist someone for; also a noun)
@card spontaneity | **tính tự phát; ứng biến tự nhiên** · n. /ˌspɒntəˈneɪəti/ — Acting naturally without detailed advance planning. | A little spontaneity made the weekend more enjoyable. (Uncountable; spontaneous (adj.))
@card varnish | **lớp véc ni** · n. /ˈvɑːnɪʃ/ — A coating that protects a surface and can make it shiny. | The table needs another coat of varnish. (a coat/layer of varnish)
